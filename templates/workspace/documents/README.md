# Your documents

Put here everything the agent should read before it builds your profile. Nothing in this folder leaves your computer, and git ignores it.

- Your CV, in every version you have (PDF, Word, or text). An older or longer version often has details the latest one dropped.
- Cover letters you have written before. The agent uses them to learn how you write; the facts in them still need your yes before they go into the profile.
- Your LinkedIn profile as a PDF: on your profile page, "More" (or "Resources"), then "Save to PDF".
- Certificates, course or degree transcripts, a portfolio, references, performance reviews: anything that shows what you did.

You can add files at any time and ask the agent to "update my profile from my documents".
